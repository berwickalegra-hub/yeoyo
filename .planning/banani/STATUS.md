- [x] **Real bug fix — every icon in the app rendering as a solid black filled silhouette instead of a thin colored outline** (2026-08-13, user-reported via screenshots of the TopNav and Accueil dashboard: house/search/eye/heart/users/shield/gem/layers icons all appearing solid black regardless of their intended `text-*` color class, only the Premium crown/gold icon looking correctly tinted).
